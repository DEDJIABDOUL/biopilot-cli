# biopilot uninstall

Remove BioPilot entirely.

```text
biopilot uninstall [--yes]
```

BioPilot lists everything it will remove and asks for confirmation:

- the program;
- its settings;
- its data folder, Profiles and usage included;
- the start line in your shell's startup files (your other lines are kept).

The rest of your files are not touched.

## Options

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| `--yes` | skip the confirmation |
